## Product meaning of this map

The point of this landscape is to show that Avenra UI is expanding by coherent product areas:

- first the field and selection foundation
- then higher-value input surfaces
- then broader overlay and data-display depth

This is how the library becomes credible at scale: not by adding names randomly, but by strengthening the structure of the surface.
